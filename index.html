A WARM WELCOME

A little world full of possibility.

Gorizont is a children's health and recreation camp in Klin, Moscow Region. Surrounded by nature, children spend their days making friends, exploring new interests, playing sports and creating memories together.

Our team creates a welcoming atmosphere where every child can feel included, discover something new and simply enjoy being a child.

We can’t wait to meet you.
